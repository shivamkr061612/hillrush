Privacy Policy

Hill Rush: Desi Racer
Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hill Rush: Desi Racer ("the Game", package com.shibuu.hillrush), made by Shibuu ("we", "us"), handles information. In short: we do not collect your personal data ourselves. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to serve and measure those ads, as explained below.

1. Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress (coins, cars, upgrades, best distances, settings and daily reward streak) is saved only on your device. It is never uploaded to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show banner ads on menus, full-screen ads between some runs, and rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch for in-game rewards.

To deliver, personalise (where allowed), limit the frequency of and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process information from your device, including: the device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and app-set ID; IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it; device model, operating system and language; app and ad interaction data (for example ads viewed or tapped); and crash and performance diagnostics of the ads SDK.

This information is collected and handled by Google under Google's own policies. Learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The list of ad technology providers used by AdMob is published at https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903.

Data sent to Google by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

3. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Legal & Info > Ad Privacy Choices. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Where applicable, the Game also supports the privacy choices required by US state privacy laws through the same Google consent tools.

4. Your choices: personalised ads

You can opt out of personalised ads on Android at any time: open your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads), then choose "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation". You can also manage Google ad settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

Opting out does not remove ads; it means the ads you see are less related to your interests.

5. Children

The Game is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at techshivam0616@gmail.com and we will help.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your game data is stored only on your device, you can delete all of it at any time by clearing the Game's app data (Android Settings > Apps > Hill Rush > Storage > Clear data) or by uninstalling the Game.

To reset the advertising identifier used by Google, use "Delete advertising ID" as described above. For any request about data handled by Google, see Google's privacy tools at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

If you have a question or a deletion request, email techshivam0616@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Permissions

The Game uses the Internet permission (to load ads) and the advertising ID permission (used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK). It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

8. Security

We do not run servers that store your data. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted over encrypted connections.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes are posted in the Game and at the policy's web address, with a new "Last updated" date.
