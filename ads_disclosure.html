Ads Disclosure

Hill Rush: Desi Racer
Last updated: October 31, 2026

This game shows ads by Google AdMob, including rewarded video ads you choose to watch for in-game rewards. Rewards have no real-money value. There are no in-app purchases.

Where ads appear

Banner ads: at the bottom of menu screens only (home, garage, worlds). Never during a run.

Full-screen ads: sometimes when you leave the results screen, at most once every few runs and never more often than every two minutes.

Rewarded video ads (optional, marked with a yellow AD tag): Revive after a crash, double the coins of a run, double the daily reward, Free Coins on the home screen (with a cooldown and a daily limit), and a Free Fuel refill once per run when fuel is low.

How rewarded ads work

You choose to watch them. The reward is given only after the video is watched to the end. If you close the ad early, no reward is given. If no ad is available, you can try again later.
